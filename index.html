Smart Lock DFA

By Easala Jagadeesh • Formal Languages and Automata Theory (FLAT)

A digital keypad lock must open as soon as the trailing keystrokes match the secret code. A naive greedy lock that restarts to 0 on any mismatch fails on overlapping sequences. A Deterministic Finite Automaton (DFA) preserves partial matches.
